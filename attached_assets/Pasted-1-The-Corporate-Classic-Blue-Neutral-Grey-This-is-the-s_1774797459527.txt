Key Principles for Portfolio Color Choice
Grey is the most important color: Use grey (or light grey) for less important elements, gridlines, or context data to make your highlight colors stand out.
Accessibility First: 8% of men have red-green color blindness. Use Viz Palette or Coblis to test your portfolio colors. Avoid red and green together—use Blue and Orange instead.
Limit to 5–7 Colors: Too many colors overwhelm the eye.
Consistency: If blue represents "Revenue" in the first chart, it must be blue in all subsequent charts.
Build in Grayscale: If your chart does not clearly show the insight in black and white, color will not fix it. Add color sparingly for emphasis.
